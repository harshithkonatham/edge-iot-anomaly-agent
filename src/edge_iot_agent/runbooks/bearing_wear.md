# Runbook: bearing wear

**Signature:** vibration (RMS velocity) rises steadily over hours; temperature may rise a few
degrees from friction; current is mostly unchanged. Usually affects one motor only.

**Severity guide (ISO 10816-style, small motors):** below 2.8 mm/s good · 2.8-4.5 mm/s
acceptable · 4.5-7.1 mm/s unsatisfactory · above 7.1 mm/s unacceptable.

**Actions**
1. Confirm with a handheld vibration analyser; check for bearing defect frequencies.
2. Check lubrication: re-grease if the schedule is overdue.
3. Inspect alignment and coupling; check mounting bolts for looseness.
4. Above 4.5 mm/s: schedule a bearing replacement in the next planned stop.
5. Above 7.1 mm/s or rising fast: stop the motor to avoid shaft or stator damage.
