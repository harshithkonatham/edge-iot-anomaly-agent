# Runbook: intermittent electrical fault

**Signature:** short, repeated current spikes (often 1.5-2.5× normal) while temperature and
vibration stay near normal. Points to a loose connection, damaged cable, failing contactor or
early insulation breakdown.

**Actions**
1. Lock out and tag out before any inspection.
2. Thermal-image terminal boxes and contactors for hot spots.
3. Check and re-torque terminal connections; inspect cables for damage.
4. Run an insulation resistance (megger) test on the windings.
5. If spikes are frequent or growing, take the motor out of service: risk of trip or fire.
