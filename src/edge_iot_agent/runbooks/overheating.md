# Runbook: motor overheating

**Signature:** winding/housing temperature climbs well above its baseline; current often rises
too as efficiency drops. Usually one motor; if the whole fleet warms up together, suspect
ambient temperature or the cooling system instead.

**Severity guide (class F insulation, housing sensor):** baseline + 10 °C watch ·
above 70 °C high · above 80 °C critical, stop to protect insulation.

**Actions**
1. Check the cooling fan and air path; clear blocked vents and fins.
2. Compare with neighbouring motors to rule out an ambient or HVAC problem.
3. Check load: an overloaded or jammed driven machine raises current and heat.
4. Check supply voltage balance across phases.
5. Above 80 °C: stop the motor and let it cool before inspection.
